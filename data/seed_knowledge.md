## DarGlobal overview
Source: https://www.luxhabitat.ae/developers/dar-global/
Company: darglobal
Dar Global PLC is a luxury real estate developer founded in 2017 as the international arm of Saudi Arabia's Dar Al Arkan. It is headquartered in Dubai, UAE, and became publicly listed on the London Stock Exchange in 2023 (ticker DAR LN). It was originally established to develop assets outside Saudi Arabia for Dar Al Arkan Real Estate Development, and was first named Dar Al Arkan International Real Estate Development.

## DarGlobal business model and markets
Source: https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/dar-global-launches-over-19bln-in-global-luxury-real-estate-projects-in-2024-ql1qndgg
Company: darglobal
Dar Global focuses predominantly on second homes for internationally mobile customers in desirable locations across the GCC and Europe, including downtown Dubai and Muscat. In 2024 it unveiled ten landmark projects across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Oman and Spain with a combined value above US$1.9 billion. It serves a clientele from over 100 nationalities and planned expansion into the United States in 2025.

## DarGlobal five-year milestone (June 2026)
Source: https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026-06-16:newsml_Zaw36YXmz:0-zawya-dar-global-marks-five-years-of-global-expansion-with-23bln-development-portfolio-and-a-bold-vision-for-the-future/
Company: darglobal
Marking its fifth anniversary, Dar Global describes itself as a USD 23 billion global real estate platform serving investors from more than 125 nationalities. Gross development value tripled from USD 7.5 billion (FY2024) to USD 23 billion (FY2025). Revenue rose 124% to USD 539 million, EBITDA reached a record USD 126 million, and cash was USD 702 million as of December 2025. A USD 250 million financing facility was recently secured. About 6,100 units are under construction across 16 projects, with five major master-planned communities in progress in Muscat, Riyadh, Jeddah and Southern Spain, and a large community planned in Greece. Note: the press release's own boilerplate cites different unit and country counts, so figures vary by source and date.

## DarGlobal Saudi Arabia expansion
Source: https://darglobal.co.uk/dar-globals-expansion-into-saudi-arabia-to-benefit-from-growing-international-interest-in-the-kingdoms-real-estate-market/
Company: darglobal
On 13 November 2023 Dar Global announced it would expand into Saudi Arabia, citing Vision 2030 and giga projects in real estate and hospitality that attract affluent international visitors. It can draw on parent company Dar Al Arkan, which has operated in Saudi Arabia for about 29 years. As of 30 June 2023 its portfolio GDV was US$5.0 billion across 11 active projects in the UK, Spain, UAE, Oman, Qatar and Bosnia. In December 2024 the CEO said Saudi projects since entry were worth SAR 2.8 billion, and that foreign buyers can purchase property of at least SAR 4 million.

## DarGlobal construction starts: Tierra Viva, AIDA, Les Vagues
Source: https://www.zawya.com/en/business/real-estate/dar-globals-three-luxury-projects-enter-construction-phase-krc2pz4g

In December 2023 Dar Global began construction on Tierra Viva in Spain, AIDA in Oman and Les Vagues in Qatar. Tierra Viva is its first project in continental Europe, comprises 53 villas and is inspired by the design of Automobili Lamborghini. AIDA is a joint venture with Omran Group, Oman's tourism development arm, a premium mixed-use project on a hilltop more than 130 m above sea level.

## Wasalt overview
Source: https://wasalt.sa/en
Company: wasalt
Wasalt is the first AI-powered real estate platform in Saudi Arabia, covering residential and commercial properties for sale and rent. Listings are verified by REGA (the General Real Estate Authority). It is a joint venture between Dar Al Arkan and Quara Holding. It is available as a website and as iOS and Android apps, with multi-language AI-generated content. Support phone: 8001233227. Tabs include Buy, Rent, Projects and Daily Rentals (new).

## Wasalt features and services
Source: https://wasalt.sa/en
Company: wasalt
Wasalt offers property search with filters (type, rooms, budget, price range), houses, villas, apartments, plots, shops and offices for sale or rent. The Matchmaker asks a questionnaire and assigns a real estate broker who contacts the user. The Communities feature lets users explore master communities and book plots online. Auctions are run in partnership with Infath (Entrustment and Liquidation Center). The AI layer, built with propgenius.ai, predicts property prices from market trends and estimates return on investment. Loan partners offer home financing. Interactive maps support quick navigation between districts and cities.

## Wasalt milestones and licences
Source: https://wasalt.sa/en/corporate
Company: wasalt
Wasalt launched in January 2022, followed by Wasalt Auction, Wasalt Business (described as the first real estate app in Saudi Arabia for brokers), Matchmaker Packages and an AI tool. It passed 15,000 registered brokers on the Wasalt Business app and 250,000 registered users in March 2024. It is licensed by Infath as a real estate auction platform and by REGA as a real estate portal.

## Wasalt PropGenius AI and Wasalt Business
Source: https://wasalt.sa/en/corporate
Company: wasalt
The PropGenius AI suite provides personalized property descriptions in several languages, social media posts based on current trends, real-time property valuations, ROI predictions and lead generation. Wasalt Business is a CRM application for brokers to manage tasks, listings, leads and teams.

## Wasalt growth, auctions and Christie's partnership
Source: https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/christies-international-real-estate-launches-in-the-kingdom-of-saudi-arabia-yrzfmz87
Company: wasalt
Wasalt is a subsidiary of Quara Holding Group and offers AI-driven property search, client-agent matchmaking, auctions and other services on one platform. Christie's International Real Estate launched in Saudi Arabia in partnership with Wasalt; Ziad Chaar is named as Wasalt's Chairman in that release, which also noted a filing for an IPO on the Saudi Parallel Market. Wasalt's Infath license lets it run electronic and hybrid real estate auctions with real-time bidding and direct award.

## How DarGlobal and Wasalt are related
Source: https://wasalt.sa/en
Company: wasalt
Both are linked to Dar Al Arkan. Dar Global is Dar Al Arkan's international luxury real estate development arm, while Wasalt is a property platform formed as a joint venture between Dar Al Arkan and Quara Holding. Dar Global targets international buyers of luxury branded residences; Wasalt serves the Saudi market with listings, projects and auctions.
